# Asset sources

No third-party image or model assets are bundled. Planet surfaces and Saturn's ring texture are generated deterministically by code into `Assets/Textures/Procedural/` as lightweight illustrative fallbacks. They are not satellite photographs or cartographic data. Materials are generated in `Assets/Materials/`; the scene plane material is a locally generated translucent cyan material.

Scientific reference data (not a downloaded visual asset): [NASA GSFC NSSDCA Planetary Fact Sheet (metric)](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/) for diameter, mass, mean distance, periods, and officially confirmed moon counts; [NASA GSFC fact sheet notes](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/planetfact_notes.html) explain definitions. The values and counts are approximate/reference values; moon counts reflect the source's 18 March 2025 update and may change.
