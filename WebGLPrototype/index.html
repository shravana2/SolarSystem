A NOTE ON SCALE

One scale, enormous distances

Planet diameters and mean orbital distances use the same scale, set by the Sun diameter. Changing the Sun diameter scales every body and orbit together. At 1×, the animation advances at real-time seconds. Higher speed settings multiply the same clock for orbital motion and axial rotation, preserving each body’s period ratios.

Enable planet pointers to locate subpixel worlds. Select a world while paused to focus the camera, or use “Zoom to body” in its information card. While paused and focused, drag the selected body to inspect its surface from different angles. Zoom and pan keep the whole model on the same scale; other worlds may move out of view as you approach one.

In top and lateral views, toggle the orbital grid to compare spacing in 1 AU steps. The labels mark selected distances from the Sun. Size comparison shows diameters only and omits orbital distances.
